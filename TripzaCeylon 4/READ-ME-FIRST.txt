TripzaCeylon — read this first
==============================

Two folders. The split matters.

  1-UPLOAD-THESE-TO-GITHUB/
      Everything your website needs. Open this folder, select ALL of it,
      and drag it into GitHub. Drag the CONTENTS, never the folder itself —
      dragging the folder is the single most common way to end up with a 404.

      .nojekyll is hidden on a Mac. Press Cmd + Shift + . to reveal it.

  2-KEEP-THESE-PRIVATE/
      Your working documents. Two of them genuinely should not be public:

        LEGAL-RISKS.md  records that the site was carrying accreditation
                        claims that were not true. A public repo would be
                        publishing that admission.

        SECURITY.md     lists the security gaps that remain, which is a
                        map for anyone wanting to poke at your site.

Start with CHECKLIST.md — one page, everything on it.
Then START-HERE.md if you want each step spelled out with button names.
